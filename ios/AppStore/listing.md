## Description

The Hub is the member app for Agentic AI UW, a student AI club at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

Sign in with your club email and see:
• Your tasks, grouped by when they're due, with urgent ones marked
• Your team meetings and the club's events, with the time, place and agenda
• The full hub website, already signed in

Your tasks go in a Reminders list called "The Hub" and your meetings go in a calendar called "The Hub". Check a task off in Reminders and it's marked done in the hub.

You need to be on the Agentic AI UW roster to sign in. Ask an exec to add you.
